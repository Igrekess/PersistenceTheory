## Not a retrodiction

A theory reproducing 43 observables to 0.30 % is interesting. A theory
that on top **predicts** 28 falsifiable quantities, with no way to fit
them after the fact, is a different category. This is PT.

> Predictive ratio: R_PT = 43:1 (43 observables reproduced from 1 input
> $s = 1/2$). Compare with the standard Standard Model, R_SM ≈ 2:1.

## The 2032 Grand Crossroads

DUNE will have to measure **simultaneously** three PMNS predictions:

$$
\delta_{\rm CP} = 197.358° \quad \text{(P4)}
$$
$$
\sin^2\theta_{23} = 0.5733 \quad \text{(P6)}
$$
$$
\sin^2\theta_{13} = 0.0222 \quad \text{(P7)}
$$

If all three are confirmed within 1 %, the probability of coincidence
is roughly $(0.01)^3 = 10^{-6}$. A rare test in physics: three falsifiable
predictions descending from **zero fitted parameters**.

## A transparent exception: P20

One prediction (P20, dark-energy equation of state $w_0, w_a$) was
**downgraded in 2026-04** from [PRED-cand] to [EXPL] (exploratory). The
reason: the initial formula produces the right structure but lacks a
canonical Lagrangian derivation, and the DESI DR2 tension on $w_0$ reaches
$3.78\sigma$.

> PT publishes the downgrade. No theory would do this if it were fitting.
> The status will be reassessed after DESI DR3 (2027), Euclid (2028),
> CMB-S4 (2030).
